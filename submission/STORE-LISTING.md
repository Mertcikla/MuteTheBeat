## Detailed description — paste into the listing

Reduce music volume from stream without affecting speech.

MuteTheBeat enhances speech in a Twitch stream so background music and other background sounds can become quieter while voices remain audible.

Open a Twitch stream, click the extension, and enable speech focus. Adjust Music reduction to find the balance you like, control output volume, and switch between Original and Enhanced to hear the difference. Close the popup and keep watching—processing continues until you disable it or close the tab.

• Adjustable music reduction and speech focus.
• One-click Original / Enhanced comparison.
• Local processing: no audio uploads, accounts, or subscription.
• No microphone access, audio recordings, or transcripts.
• Automatically restores original playback if processing cannot keep up.

MuteTheBeat runs its bundled speech-enhancement model on your computer. It only processes a Twitch tab after you enable it. Settings stay in Chrome's local storage. It accesses the selected tab's URL and title to validate Twitch playback and identify your active session; it does not build a browsing-history log.

Please note: this is speech enhancement, not perfect music separation. Song vocals and other speakers may remain audible. Game sounds may be reduced, and stronger settings can affect voice quality. Processing adds a small audio delay and uses CPU resources. Results depend on the stream and your computer. Start at the default setting and adjust to taste.

Requires desktop Chrome 116 or newer. Processes one Twitch tab at a time. After restarting Chrome, click Enable again. Disable restores the original stereo tab audio.

MuteTheBeat is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch or Google.
