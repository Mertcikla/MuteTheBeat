# Submit MuteTheBeat

The files are prepared for submission; nothing has been uploaded or published.

1. Sign in to your registered Chrome Web Store developer account and complete its required account/contact setup.
2. Publish `privacy.html` at a public HTTPS address you control. Use your real developer/support contact in the store account. The policy points users to that contact.
3. Create a new item and upload **`MuteTheBeat-1.0.0.zip`**. Its `manifest.json` is at the ZIP root. Do **not** upload the outer submission-kit ZIP as the extension.
4. Paste the listing copy from `STORE-LISTING.md` and upload the supplied icon, two screenshots, and small promotional tile. The marquee is optional.
5. Complete the privacy fields using `PRIVACY-AND-PERMISSIONS.md`, including local audio and limited tab-details handling. Paste the public privacy-policy URL.
6. Add `REVIEWER-NOTES.md` where review/test instructions can be supplied. No test credentials are needed.
7. Choose distribution countries and visibility in your account, preview the listing, then submit for review when ready.
