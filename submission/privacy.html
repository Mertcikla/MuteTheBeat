MuteTheBeat Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

MuteTheBeat reduces background music and other background sounds in a user-selected Twitch tab while preserving speech. Audio processing takes place on your device. The extension does not upload audio or browsing information to the developer or any third party.

Information handled locally

When you click Enable, MuteTheBeat captures the selected tab’s audio and processes short audio buffers in memory to enhance speech. It accesses the selected tab’s URL to confirm that it is a supported Twitch page, its title to identify the active session in the popup, and its tab identifier to manage that session. It does not read your browsing history, Twitch chat, login credentials, or cookies, and does not request microphone access.

Reduction strength, output volume, and Original/Enhanced preference are saved in Chrome’s local extension storage. Temporary session information, audio buffers, and performance measurements are kept in memory while the session is running. The extension does not record or save audio files, create transcripts, or train models on your audio.

Use, retention, and deletion

Information is used only to provide and control speech enhancement. Clicking Disable stops capture and releases the audio session. Closing the tab or browser also ends the session. Audio buffers and session information are discarded when the session ends. Preferences remain on the device until changed or until the extension is uninstalled. Uninstalling the extension removes its local preferences.

No data transmission, sale, or advertising

MuteTheBeat has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, account system, or remote processing service. Its executable code and model are bundled with the extension. The developer receives no captured audio, tab details, or preferences through the extension, does not sell or share them, and does not use them for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

Your browser’s ordinary communications with Twitch and the Chrome Web Store operate independently of MuteTheBeat and are governed by those services’ policies. If you voluntarily contact support, the information you choose to send is handled through that support channel rather than collected by the extension.

Limited Use

MuteTheBeat’s use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Such information is used only for the extension’s visible speech-enhancement functionality.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on MuteTheBeat’s Chrome Web Store listing. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in this policy and disclosed in the extension before taking effect where required.
